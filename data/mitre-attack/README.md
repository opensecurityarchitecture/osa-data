# MITRE ATT&CK, as OSA uses it

Two files, each generated by `scripts/build_attack_extract.py` from one published source. Do not edit them by hand: change the source or the script and rebuild.

- **`enterprise-attack.json`**: MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise v20.0 (13 October 2026), from MITRE's own STIX data. The 15 tactics in the order of MITRE's matrix; 227 techniques and 479 sub-techniques, with their tactics, platforms, parent and the first paragraph of MITRE's description; 178 groups with the techniques MITRE says they use; 44 mitigations and 703 detection strategies with the techniques they cover. Techniques MITRE has revoked or deprecated are left out.
- **`sp800-53-to-attack.json`**: the Center for Threat-Informed Defense's mapping of NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 controls to the techniques they mitigate (Mappings Explorer), made for ATT&CK 16.1 and carried to v20.0. CTID has published no SP 800-53 mapping for a later ATT&CK version.

The source addresses and the checksums of the copies used are in each file under `source`.

## What was changed in CTID's mapping

- **Carried to ATT&CK v20.0.** Where MITRE revoked a technique after 16.1, the pair moves to the successor MITRE names in its revoked-by relationship, and keeps the id CTID used under `technique_in_source`. Fifteen techniques were carried this way, most of them Impair Defenses (T1562), which v19 split up.
- **One pair dropped**: SC-12 with T1521.003, a Mobile technique that is not part of Enterprise. Dropped pairs are listed under `checks.dropped`.
- **Duplicates merged.** CTID's file repeats some pairs, and some carried pairs meet an existing one. 5,264 distinct pairs in the source become 5,236.
- CTID's comments are not carried, except on techniques it marks non-mappable (`non_mappable`, 96 techniques).

## What the coverage figure means

466 of the 706 techniques and sub-techniques have at least one SP 800-53 control in this mapping. That says CTID mapped a control to the technique. It does not measure how well any organisation, or any OSA pattern, defends against it. Of the other 240, CTID marks 96 as non-mappable; the remaining 144 are newer than CTID's mapping or were left out of it. MITRE's own disclaimer applies: using ATT&CK to cover full categories of techniques "will not guarantee full defensive coverage".
